# How do you define [romantic] love?

Recently, my girlfriend and I had a dicussion in the car that has made me start thinking about love again. Both in the sense that I think I'm falling in love with her, as well as just thinking about love as a concept. How each person defines love is probably different, and in my opinion, it's hard to truly give it a "definition". Rather, I think it's a whole bunch of little pieces put together that create love.

I think my definition of love is shaped by my past experiences - as it probably is for everyone. Previously, I made the mistake of thinking lust or desire was love, when in reality they are small components of it.

I think the main component for me is **a sense of belonging and trust**. That extremely warm feeling of being comfortable - I mean SO comfortable - with someone that it truly feels like you belong together. When you feel like you are happy with just existing together and then everything else exciting and complicated and tough builds on top of that. 

Time has helped me to understand that the sense of belonging only matters to the two people involved. Others' opinions should not be considered. I spent a lot of time thinking about what others might think of me in relation to my relationships. Would someone approve of a gf? What would old friends think if they saw me marry this person?

I don't think that way anymore. I'm confident in my thoughts and have become intentional about seeking out love. It's now about what I feel, not what others may think.
